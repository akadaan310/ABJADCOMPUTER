# ٠٣ — مُقلِع الأبجدية الأولى: آب جد هو زح
# 03 — The primordial alphabet bootloader

> Generated by the ABJADCOMPUTER engine. Every value below is a sum of
> table look-ups and can be re-checked with pencil and paper.


The received order of the alphabet is not alphabetical but *numerical*: its first eight letters carry the units 1–8 in sequence, and they are memorised in pairs. Those pairs are the engine's four inherited operations.

## 2. Phrasal segmentation

| pair | letters | sum | abjad numeral | opcode | meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| آب | ا(1) + ب(2) | 3 | ج | PARENT | الأب / parent |
| جد | ج(3) + د(4) | 7 | ز | GRANDPARENT | الجد / grandparent |
| هو | ه(5) + و(6) | 11 | يا | IDENTITY | هو / identity |
| زح | ز(7) + ح(8) | 15 | يه | SHIFT | الزحزحة / displacement |

```
آب : 1 + 2 =  3  -> ج
جد : 3 + 4 =  7  -> ز
هو : 5 + 6 = 11  -> يا
زح : 7 + 8 = 15  -> يه
```

## 3. Two facts that make this a machine rather than a mnemonic

**(a) The sums are an arithmetic progression of stride 4.**

```
3, 7, 11, 15    differences: 4, 4, 4
```

Consecutive disjoint pairs of consecutive integers always advance by 4, so the four operations are separated by a constant. That is why an address reduced **modulo 4** selects exactly one of them, and why the operation table has four entries and not some other number:

| address mod 4 | opcode | effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | IDENTITY | hold position |
| 1 | PARENT | ascend one level |
| 2 | GRANDPARENT | ascend two levels |
| 3 | SHIFT | displace to next container member |

**(b) The first two sums stay inside the octet; the last two do not.**

| pair | sum | representable as one letter? | rendering |
|---|---|---|---|
| آب | 3 | yes | ج |
| جد | 7 | yes | ز |
| هو | 11 | no — carries | يا  (11) |
| زح | 15 | no — carries | يه  (15) |

آب→ج and جد→ز land back on letters that are themselves *inside* the source octet (positions 3 and 7). هو→11 and زح→15 exceed 9 and must be written with two cells, يا and يه. The alphabet's own opening therefore demonstrates a register file of eight single-cell slots plus a carry discipline: values that overflow the unit range spill into a two-cell composite whose high cell is ي (10).

## 4. The memory allocation table

Allocating one slot per pair and letting the carry rule decide its width:

| pair | value | numeral | cells | allocation | opcode | hash address |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| آب | 3 | ج | 1 | unit register | PARENT | `J3/D0/P0/S0` |
| جد | 7 | ز | 1 | unit register | GRANDPARENT | `J7/D0/P0/S0` |
| هو | 11 | يا | 2 | carry pair (high ي = 10) | IDENTITY | `J11/D0/P0/S0` |
| زح | 15 | يه | 2 | carry pair (high ي = 10) | SHIFT | `J15/D0/P0/S0` |

Total allocated: **36** = لو — which is also 1+2+…+8, the sum of the whole octet. The allocation is exact: nothing in the first eight letters is left unassigned and nothing is double-counted.

## 5. Inheritance and state transition

The four opcodes are precisely a tree-walk instruction set:

```
آب  PARENT       node -> node.parent            (one level up)
جد  GRANDPARENT  node -> node.parent.parent     (two levels up)
هو  IDENTITY     node -> node                   (hold)
زح  SHIFT        node -> next member of its container  (lateral jump)
```

Three of them move on **tree** edges (up, up-twice, stay); the fourth moves on a **graph** edge (sideways, through the constellation container). The primordial order thus supplies both link systems of the hybrid structure — vertical inheritance from آب/جد/هو, horizontal addressing from زح.

## 6. Distribution over the corpus

Reducing every line of the Jazariyyah modulo 4 gives the opcode each line executes:

| opcode | lines | share |
|---|---|---|
| IDENTITY | 25 | 22% |

All four operations are exercised; no line of the poem is unreachable.
