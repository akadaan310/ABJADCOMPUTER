## 1. The two coordinates

```
digital root   dr(J) = 1 + (J-1) mod 9        9 classes   -- the 'declination'
ring position  r(J)  = 1 + (J-1) mod 28       28 classes  -- the 'right ascension'
container      C<dr>.<r>                      up to 252 cells
```

Both are hand-computable: dr by casting out nines, r by casting out twenty-eights. Two nodes agreeing on both are one hop apart regardless of where they sit in the tree.
