## 5. Why the paradox is visible in print at all

The two witnesses collated for this repository order the closing lines differently, and that difference is the whole mechanism:

| ordinal | witness A (takw.in, primary) | witness B (Wikisource) |
|---|---|---|
| 107 | 107 أبياتها قاف وزاي في العدد | 107 والحمد لله لها ختام |
| 108 | 108 والحمد لله لها ختام | 108 على النبي المصطفى محمدا |
| 109 | 109 على النبي المصطفى وآله | 109 أبياتها قاف وزاي بالعدد |

In **A** the colophon closes the poem at ordinal 107 and the two doxology lines follow it as an appendix; the statement is true of its own position and nothing looks wrong. In **B** the same two lines are inserted *before* the colophon, displacing it to ordinal 109 — and a line reading *“its verses are 107”* sitting at position 109 is exactly the contradiction readers report.

The paradox is therefore not arithmetical but **positional**: it is produced by where the appendix was spliced, not by any error in the count.
